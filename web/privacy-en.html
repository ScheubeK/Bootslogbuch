Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Controller

Bootslogbuch is operated by Karl Scheuber. For privacy questions contact k.scheuber@gmail.com.

2. Purpose

Bootslogbuch provides a shared digital boat logbook. Data is processed solely to provide accounts, boats, crew memberships, trips, costs, optional cost sharing, reports, backups and restoration.

3. Data processed

Depending on use, we process email address, display name and sign-in data. A boat may contain trip data (times, engine hours, weather, air and water temperatures, notes and wine entries) and cost data (date, category, description, amount and optional fuel information). When cost sharing is enabled, payer, participating members, cost shares and settlements are also stored.

4. Access within a boat

Crew members invited by the captain can view and edit the relevant boat data according to their role. Other users cannot access that boat’s data.

5. Service providers

  • Cloudflare Pages: website hosting.
  • Supabase: user authentication and data storage.
  • Google: optional Google OAuth sign-in.
  • Open-Meteo: weather data for the configured weather location or its coordinates.
  • Alplakes / Eawag: a modelled water-temperature value for the selected lake at 1 m depth. The request is routed through our Cloudflare function; no account, boat or personal location data is sent.

Technical content from third parties, such as Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, SheetJS and the live-trip animation, may process IP address and browser data as technically necessary.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is retained while the related account and boat exist, or until authorised users delete it. The captain can delete trips, costs and settlements for one year or the whole boat. Account deletion can be requested by email.

7. Local storage and no advertising

Bootslogbuch uses browser storage to retain the sign-in session, selected boat and language. No advertising or analytics trackers are used.

8. Your rights

You may request information, correction or deletion of your stored personal data. Depending on applicable law, further rights may apply.

The German version of this privacy policy is authoritative.
