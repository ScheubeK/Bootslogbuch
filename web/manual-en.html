User guide

From your first account to an annual review: the most important features in a few simple steps.

1. Sign in, create an account and boat

  1. Choose Sign in with Google, or use email and password.
  2. If you are new, choose Create an account.
  3. When creating your first boat, enter its name. You become its captain automatically.
  4. If you were invited to a crew, use the same email address and open the invitation link after signing in.

2. Record a trip

Open Trips and choose Add trip. Enter times, engine hours and notes. With Live tracking, the start time is safely retained even after iPhone sleep; switch it off at the end and save the completed trip. Weather and water values are suggestions and can always be changed.

3. Dashboard and years

The dashboard opens on the current year. Change the year at the top to view previous seasons. In Settings → Customize dashboard, choose the metric tiles you want to see. The season review can be shared.

4. Costs and sharing

Use Add expense for fuel, insurance, taxes, mooring and maintenance. Litres and price per litre are optional for fuel. If enabled, Split this cost lets you select the payer and participants; the cost-sharing view shows balances and settlement suggestions.

5. Settings, crew and backups

The captain invites or removes crew members. Weather location & lake controls automatic weather and water-temperature suggestions. Data export & import creates or restores an Excel backup. Delete data lets the captain delete one year or all logbook data for the boat.
