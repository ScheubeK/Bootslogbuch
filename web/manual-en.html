User guide

From your first account to an annual review: the most important features in a few simple steps.

The screen views use neutral example data. Some areas look different depending on your role: the captain manages the boat and crew; all crew members can record data in the shared boat logbook.

1. Getting started

Sign in, account and boat

  1. Choose Sign in with Google, or sign in with your email address and password.
  2. New here? Choose Create an account.
  3. For your first boat, enter the boat name. You automatically become the captain.
  4. If you were invited to a crew, register or sign in with the same email address and then open the invitation link.

2. On the water

Record a trip

Open Trips and tap Add trip. Start and end times, engine hours and notes can always be entered manually.

  • Live tracking: turn the switch on before you leave. The start remains saved even after your iPhone sleeps. When you finish, complete the details and save the trip.
  • Weather, air temperature, wind and water temperature are suggested automatically. You can overwrite every value.
  • The wine switch displays the bottle selection.

3. Keep an overview

Dashboard and years

The dashboard automatically shows the current year. Use the year selector to look at previous years at any time.

  • Under Settings → Customize dashboard, choose the metric tiles you want to see.
  • Cost categories and the cost trend across years remain visible below.
  • The Season review summarizes the selected year and can be shared.

4. Organize expenses

Costs and cost sharing

Use Add expense to record fuel, insurance, taxes, mooring and maintenance. Litres and price per litre are optional for fuel only.

  • When the feature is enabled, select Split this cost, then choose the payer and participants.
  • In Split costs, you can see balances, total shared costs and suggested settlements.
  • The captain decides who is generally included in shared costs.

5. Manage and back up

Settings, crew and data

  • The captain invites additional people by email under Crew and can remove them there again.
  • Under Weather location & lake, choose the location for weather data and the lake used for automatic water temperature.
  • Data export & import creates an Excel backup with trips, costs and, if used, cost sharing. Import adds the data to the current boat.
  • Delete data lets the captain remove one selected year or all logbook data for the boat.
  • Use the three-dot menu to create another boat of your own or switch between your boats.

One more tip

Each boat has its own shared data space. If you are crew on one boat and later want a boat of your own, use the menu to choose Create own boat at any time. Your existing boat memberships remain unchanged.
